## Lessons carried (macOS)

- `GOARCH=386` alone fails on darwin: `GOOS=linux GOARCH=386 make build`;
  `make test-386` runs on CI only.
- Stale `ui/node_modules` after a dependabot bump: `make web-install`
  before `dist-check` / `notices-check`; on ENOTEMPTY
  `rm -rf ui/node_modules` first.
- npm pins are exact: `--save-exact`; `peerDependencies` stay.
- zsh does not split `$VAR`: no `set -- $M`.
- `gmake` where BSD make 3.81 misreads `#` in `$(shell …)`.
