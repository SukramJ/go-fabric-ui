## Report shape

`phase-1b-<unit>.md`: per finding ID, severity (high / medium / low / note),
`file:line`, claim, measured (M: read or run) or inferred (I), proposed fix,
whether the fix changes the contract (decision 13), a red test in words.
Already known from phase 0 and 1a is not re-reported (`phase-0-register.md`,
`phase-1a-register.md`). Reports are written to the git-excluded `held/`
directory first; the public copy is made by the coordinator after
verification, without the hints to held rows.
