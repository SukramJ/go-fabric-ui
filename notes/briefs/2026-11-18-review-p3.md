## Rules for every reviewer

- **Not re-reported**: the rows of the four registers and of the carry-over
  inventory, and the held rows. A new path or a wider reach of a known row
  is a new finding that names the old ID.
- **Decided shapes** (decisions 37–44) are the target, not a finding: a
  reviewer reports where the tree or the docs will contradict them, or
  what they miss.
- **Show it.** A finding of severity low or above on code carries a test
  that fails today, written and run in a scratch worktree, never in the
  review worktree; a docs, citation or workflow finding cites both sides'
  lines instead. Measured (M) and inferred (I) are reported apart.
- **Held material**: three rows are held (decisions 18, 25). A finding of
  their class is a **held candidate** and says so on its first line.
- Severity: high (a role gets a secret or an admin action it must not, or
  a host is misled into an unsafe setup), medium (a real defect with a
  precondition), low, note.
- Contract (decision 13): say yes/no for each fix.

## Report shape

`notes/review/2026-11-17/held/phase-3-<unit>.md` (the coordinator publishes
a redacted copy): per finding ID, severity, `file:line`, claim, M/I, test
or both sides' lines, proposed fix, contract yes/no; then "Covered without
finding".
